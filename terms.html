“Rehab” is an app that teaches Arabic letters to children.

التطبيق مخصّص للتعلّم واللعب تحت إشراف ولي الأمر. يرجى استخدامه بما يناسب عمر الطفل.

Subscriptions and purchases are made through the parent's Apple Account. Yearly and monthly subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date, and can be managed or cancelled in your App Store account settings at any time.
